The
Recipe
for
Dressing
Like
Undarga.

step one: choose a fun thrifted tee.

preferably colorful, but if you don't have any or if it's out of your comfort zone, you can make it work with a darker t-shirt with an interesting patterns or graphics.

step two: knee lenght skirt

this is a absolute must for this recipe! it can't be a mini skirt or maxi skirt, but a knee lenght skirt. on this step, you can lean into simpler skirts, if your shirt choice is bold. however, if your opted for a darker tee, make sure your skirt has interesting layers, patterns or materials.

step three: knee high sheer socks or tights

choose a color that is contrasting from your t-shirt color.
extra points if it has cool patterns.

step four: tabis, flats

i usually go for black ballet flats or tabis for shoes. make sure it's simple,
and perfectly balances the rest of the outfit.

step five: accessorize!

accessories will make any outfit much better, so don't think this step is optional. silver earrings, rings and bracelets or a watch. pins, buttons, hair clips or a scarf are optional. but no necklaces! and lastly a black hand bag that goes with everything.

great job!
